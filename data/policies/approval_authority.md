# Approval Authority Matrix (mock, v2026.10)

### APR-1.1 Claims Officer
May approve warranty, parts, transit and dealer claims up to 25,000 INR with no policy exception and agent confidence of at least 0.85.

### APR-1.2 Claims Manager
Approves claims from 25,000 to 2,00,000 INR, every rejection, every suspected duplicate, and every policy exception regardless of amount.

### APR-1.3 Finance Controller
Approves, after the Claims Manager, any claim above 2,00,000 INR, and employee claims above 25,000 INR after the reporting manager.

### APR-1.4 Reporting Manager
Approves all employee reimbursement claims for their reports.

### APR-1.5 Quality Lead
Approves corrective-action (CAPA) tickets raised from recurring failures or dealer audit findings.

### APR-2.1 No auto-execution
No settlement, replacement, carrier recovery, corrective action or decision letter is executed without a signed approval.
