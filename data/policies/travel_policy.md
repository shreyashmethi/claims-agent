# Employee Travel and Expense Policy (mock, v2026.10)

### EMP-2.1 Submission window
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip.

### EMP-2.2 Receipts
Receipts are required for every expense of 500 INR or more, except per diem and mileage.

### EMP-2.3 Business purpose
Every claim must state the business purpose of the trip.

### EMP-4.1 Actual costs
Airfare, rail, taxi and other approved expenses are reimbursed at actual cost with a receipt.

### EMP-4.2 Hotel
Hotel is reimbursed up to the nightly cap for the employee's grade and city tier (G1: 4,000/3,000; G2: 6,000/4,500; G3: 9,000/6,500 INR for tier 1/tier 2 cities).

### EMP-4.3 Per diem
Meals and incidentals are paid as per diem by grade and city tier (G1: 2,500/1,800; G2: 3,500/2,500; G3: 5,000/3,500 INR per day).

### EMP-4.4 Mileage
Use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at 9 INR per km.
