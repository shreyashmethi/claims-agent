# Transit Damage Policy (mock, v2026.10)

### TRN-2.1 Notification window
Transit damage must be reported within 7 days of delivery. This requirement cannot be waived.

### TRN-2.2 Claim matches shipment
The serial number and dispatch on the claim must match the shipment record. Clerical errors may be corrected on documentary evidence such as a serial plate photograph.

### TRN-2.3 Damage noted on proof of delivery
The consignee must record visible damage on the proof of delivery (POD). If the POD carries no remark, the claim may be accepted only as a policy exception, on written acknowledgement of damage from the carrier, with Claims Manager approval.

### TRN-4.1 Compensation limit
Compensation is limited to the lowest of the claimed amount, the dispatch invoice value and the insured value. Amounts above the dispatch value are not paid without the original purchase invoice.

### TRN-5.1 Carrier recovery
Where the carrier acknowledges or is liable for the damage, a recovery claim for the compensated amount is filed against the carrier.
