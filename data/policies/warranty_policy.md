# Warranty Policy (mock, v2026.10)

### WAR-2.1 Coverage period
Equipment is covered for failures occurring between the warranty start date (date of sale) and the warranty end date in the unit's warranty contract. Standard plan: 24 months.

### WAR-2.2 Operating-hours cap
Coverage ends when the unit's hour meter exceeds the hours cap in the contract (standard plan: 3,000 hours), even if the coverage period has not ended. The hours reading on the dealer job card is authoritative.

### WAR-2.4 Reporting window
Warranty claims must be submitted within 30 days of the failure date. Late claims are rejected unless the Claims Manager approves an exception.

### WAR-3.1 Covered components
Only component groups listed in the contract are covered (standard: hydraulics, cooling, electrical, engine, undercarriage). Wear items, consumables and filters are excluded.

### WAR-3.4 Exclusions
Failures caused by misuse, accident, non-OEM parts or missed scheduled maintenance are excluded. Evidence of missed maintenance must come from service history.

### WAR-5.2 Failed part returned
The failed part must be returned to the manufacturer and recorded on the job card. Claims without a returned part are rejected.

### WAR-6.1 Parts reimbursement
Parts are reimbursed at the current catalog price, regardless of the price on the dealer invoice.

### WAR-6.2 Labour reimbursement
Labour is reimbursed at the dealer's tier rate (A: 1,500, B: 1,800, C: 2,100 INR per hour) for the standard hours of the job code. Hours above standard are not reimbursed.

### WAR-6.9 Non-reimbursable items
Freight, consumables and administrative charges on dealer invoices are not reimbursed under warranty.

### WAR-7.1 Settlement
Approved warranty claims are settled by credit note to the dealer account.
