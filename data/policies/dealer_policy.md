# Dealer Reimbursement Policy (mock, v2026.10)

### DLR-2.1 Authorised service
Dealers are reimbursed only for service types they are authorised for in the dealer master (field service, scheduled service, repair).

### DLR-2.2 Pre-authorisation
Claims above 50,000 INR require a pre-authorisation number issued before the work.

### DLR-2.3 Submission window
Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the service date.

### DLR-3.1 One claim per invoice
Each dealer invoice may be reimbursed once. A resubmitted invoice is rejected as a duplicate and the dealer's submission history is reviewed.

### DLR-4.1 Parts and labour
Parts at catalog price; labour at the dealer's tier rate for up to 4 standard hours of field service.

### DLR-4.3 Travel
Travel is reimbursed at 12 INR per km, up to 150 km per visit.
