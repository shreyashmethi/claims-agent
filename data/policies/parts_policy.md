# Parts Replacement Policy (mock, v2026.10)

### PRT-2.1 Return window
Claims for defective or incorrect spare parts must be raised within 30 days of the dispatch date.

### PRT-3.1 Defect or wrong part confirmed
A wrong-part claim is valid when the warehouse dispatch scan shows a part different from the part ordered and the claimant reports receiving that part. A defect claim requires photographic or inspection evidence of the defect.

### PRT-4.1 Settlement by replacement
Valid claims are settled by dispatching replacement parts at no charge, valued at catalog price. Cash refunds are not issued. The incorrect or defective part must be returned against a return authorisation.
